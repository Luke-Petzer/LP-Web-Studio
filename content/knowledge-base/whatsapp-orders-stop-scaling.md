## The point where WhatsApp breaks

Every wholesaler and distributor we've worked with started the same way: orders came in over WhatsApp. It's free, everyone has it, and for your first handful of customers it's genuinely the right tool.

Then you grow.

### The symptoms

- Orders sit in a chat thread and someone has to **re-type each one** into a spreadsheet or accounting package.
- A message gets missed, and an order simply doesn't happen.
- Custom pricing lives in someone's head, so quotes get argued out in DMs.
- Nobody can answer "what's outstanding right now?" without scrolling.

None of these are WhatsApp's fault. It was never built to be an ordering system. It's a messaging app doing a job it was never designed for.

## What replaces it

A custom ordering portal takes the same relationship — your clients, your pricing, your catalogue — and gives it structure:

- Clients log in and see **their own** pricing, not a generic list.
- They order from a live catalogue that reflects real stock.
- The moment they submit, the order routes to the warehouse and accounting automatically.

The order stops being a message someone has to act on and becomes a record the system acts on.

## When to make the move

If you're spending real hours a week re-typing orders, or you've lost an order in a thread in the last month, you're already past the point WhatsApp scales to. That's the signal it's time for a system.
