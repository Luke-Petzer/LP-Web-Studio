## Why Speed Matters More Than Ever

In today's digital landscape, **website speed isn't just a technical metric — it's a business metric.** Google has made Core Web Vitals a ranking factor, meaning slow websites are actively penalized in search results.

For Cape Town small businesses, this is especially critical. Your potential customers are searching on mobile devices, often on limited data plans. Every second of delay costs you leads.

## The Numbers Don't Lie

- **53%** of mobile visits are abandoned if a page takes longer than 3 seconds to load
- **7 out of 10** local competitors score below 90 on PageSpeed
- A **1-second delay** in page response can result in a **7% reduction** in conversions

## What Makes a Website Fast?

### 1. Modern Framework
Next.js generates static HTML at build time, eliminating the need for server-side rendering on every request. This means your pages load almost instantly.

### 2. Optimized Assets
Every image is automatically converted to modern formats (WebP/AVIF), compressed, and served from a global CDN. No more bloated 5MB hero images.

### 3. Zero Render-Blocking Resources
Unlike WordPress themes that load 20+ CSS and JavaScript files, our builds use tree-shaking and code splitting to deliver only what each page needs.

### 4. Edge Deployment
Your site is deployed to Vercel's Cape Town edge node, meaning South African visitors get sub-100ms response times instead of waiting for servers in Europe.

## How We Guarantee 90+

At LP Web Studio, every website we build undergoes rigorous performance testing before launch. We measure:

- **LCP** (Largest Contentful Paint) — Target: Under 1.2 seconds
- **INP** (Interaction to Next Paint) — Target: Under 100 milliseconds
- **CLS** (Cumulative Layout Shift) — Target: Under 0.1

If your site doesn't hit 90+ on Google PageSpeed Insights, we optimize until it does. That's our guarantee.
